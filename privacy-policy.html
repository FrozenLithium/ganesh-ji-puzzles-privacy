About this policy

Ganesh Ji Puzzles (“the app”) is a storybook puzzle game for children and families, developed by WMD Games. WMD Corporations is responsible for the app and for this policy. This policy explains what information the app handles, who receives it, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We do not receive personal information from the app. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, and no way to share pictures, voice or text. It never asks for a name, email address, phone number, home address or location, and it does not use the camera, microphone, contacts or files on the device. We run no servers for the app.

The app does include advertising and purchase services from Google and Unity Technologies. These services automatically collect some technical information from the device, as described below. That information goes to those companies, not to us.

The app has no analytics or crash-reporting service of its own, and the Unity engine’s own analytics and diagnostics services are switched off.

If you choose Send feedback in Settings, your email app opens with our address. We then receive only what you decide to send us: your email address and your message.

Game progress stored on your device

The app saves your game progress and settings on your device: finished levels and stars, sound, voice, subtitle and vibration settings, which in-app items you own, and rewards earned from optional videos. This file stays in the app’s own storage area and is not sent to us.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes this file. If Android backup is turned on, Android may keep a copy in your own Google account, managed by Google under your account settings.

Advertising

The app shows:

  • a short ad break between levels, only when moving on from the “Level Complete” screen, and not after the first level (never when the app starts, during play, or during the story);
  • a small banner ad at the bottom of the game’s Home screen only (never inside a level);
  • optional reward videos that the player chooses to watch, for example to open a level early, to get a hint, or to have no ad breaks for a while.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Because the app is made for children:

  • every ad request uses Google’s child treatment setting and asks for ads suitable for a general (G-rated) audience only. Google therefore does not show personalised (interest-based) ads or remarketing;
  • the app does not use the Android advertising ID. The permission that allows access to it has been removed, as have Android’s newer ad-tracking features (ad attribution and interest topics);
  • no other ad networks are used.

To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google’s ad software automatically collects some technical information:

  • the device’s IP address, which can indicate a general (city-level) location;
  • interactions with ads, such as views and taps;
  • app and ad performance information (diagnostics);
  • an app-specific device identifier (the “app set ID”).

This information goes to Google, not to us, and is covered by Google’s policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Each time the app starts, it uses Google’s consent tool (the User Messaging Platform) to check whether a privacy choice is needed where you live, with the setting for users under the age of consent switched on. Ads are only requested once that check allows it. Where a choice is required, you can review or change it at any time with the Privacy choices button on the game’s Settings page.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases (removing ad breaks, unlocking all puzzles, or a one-day pass without ad breaks) are made through Google Play. The app never sees or stores payment details such as card, UPI or wallet information. Google Play’s terms and privacy policy apply to the payment. Each time the shop connects, the app asks Google Play which items your Google account owns, so purchases are restored after a reinstall or on a new device.

The shop uses Unity In-App Purchasing, software by Unity Technologies. Only when a purchase is started, completed or fails, it sends Unity technical details about that purchase:

  • the product, price and currency;
  • the Google Play order reference and purchase receipt;
  • the device model, operating system version, language settings and storage size;
  • the app version and install time;
  • a random installation ID and a session ID.

It does not send your name, contact details or the advertising ID. Unity uses this for purchase analytics and to keep purchases working correctly. See Unity’s privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Children’s privacy

The app is designed for children and is built to follow Google Play’s Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The advertising and purchase services work as described above, with child settings switched on. Purchases are handled by Google Play, which can require the account holder’s password or, with Google Family Link, a parent’s approval.

If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example by email, contact us at warismd06@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Data security and retention

We store no app data on our own servers. Information handled by Google and Unity is sent encrypted (HTTPS), and those companies protect and keep it according to their own privacy policies. To ask about data held by them, use the links above. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you, and we delete them on request.

Your choices

  • The game can be played offline. Ads and the shop need an internet connection; without one, the game simply shows no ads.
  • You can clear the app’s data or uninstall the app at any time to delete your game progress from the device.
  • Privacy choices on the game’s Settings page, where shown, lets you review or change your ad-privacy choice.
  • To have an email you sent us deleted, write to warismd06@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date.
