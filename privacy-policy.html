Privacy Policy – Ganesh Ji Puzzles

Last updated: 7 October 2026
Developer: WMD Corporations
Contact: warismd06@gmail.com

About this policy

Ganesh Ji Puzzles (“the app”) is a storybook puzzle game for children and families, published by WMD Corporations. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, and no way to share pictures, voice or text. We never ask for a name, email address, phone number, home address or location. The app does not use the camera, microphone, contacts or device storage. WMD Corporations runs no servers for the app and receives no data from it.

The app does not use any analytics or crash-reporting service of its own. The Unity engine’s analytics and diagnostics features are switched off.

Game progress stored on your device

The app saves your game progress and settings on your device: finished levels and stars, sound, voice, subtitle and vibration settings, which in-app items you own, and rewards earned from optional videos. This file stays in the app’s private storage and is not sent to us.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes this file. If Android backup is turned on, Android may keep a copy in your own Google account, managed by Google under your account settings.

Advertising

The app shows:

  • a short ad break between levels, only after a level is finished (never when the app starts, during play, or during the story);
  • optional reward videos that the player chooses to watch, for example to open a level early or to have no ad breaks for a while.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Because the app is made for children:

  • every ad request is marked as child-directed. Google therefore does not show personalised (interest-based) ads or remarketing, and only shows ads suitable for a general (G-rated) audience;
  • the app does not use the Android advertising ID. The permission that allows access to it has been removed, as have Android’s newer ad-tracking features (ad attribution and interest topics);
  • no other ad networks are used.

To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google’s ad software automatically collects some technical information:

  • the device’s IP address, which can indicate a general (city-level) location;
  • interactions with ads, such as views and taps;
  • app and ad performance information;
  • an app-specific device identifier (the “app set ID”).

This information goes to Google, not to us, and is covered by Google’s policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where the law requires consent, Google’s consent tool may ask for a choice. You can change it later with the Privacy choices button in the game’s menu, which appears where required.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases (such as removing ad breaks or unlocking all puzzles) are made through Google Play. The app never sees or stores payment details such as card, UPI or wallet information. Google Play’s terms and privacy policy apply to the payment. Each time the shop connects, the app asks Google Play which items your Google account owns, so purchases are restored after a reinstall or on a new device.

The shop uses Unity In-App Purchasing, software by Unity Technologies. Only when a purchase is started, completed or fails, it sends Unity technical details about that purchase:

  • the product, price and currency;
  • the Google Play order reference and purchase receipt;
  • the device model, operating system version, language settings and storage size;
  • the app version and install time;
  • a random installation ID and a session ID.

It does not send your name, contact details or the advertising ID. Unity uses this for purchase analytics and to keep purchases working correctly. See Unity’s privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Children’s privacy

The app is designed for children and follows Google Play’s Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The advertising and purchase services work as described above, with child-directed settings switched on. Purchases need the Google Play account holder’s approval, for example a parent’s password or Family Link approval.

If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us at warismd06@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Data security and retention

We store no data on our own servers. Information handled by Google and Unity is sent encrypted (HTTPS), and those companies protect and keep it according to their own privacy policies. To ask about data held by them, use the links above.

Your choices

  • The game can be played offline. Ads and the shop need an internet connection.
  • You can clear the app’s data or uninstall the app at any time to delete your game progress from the device.
  • Privacy choices in the game’s menu, where shown, lets you review your ad-consent choice.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date.
